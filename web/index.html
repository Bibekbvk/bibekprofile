About Bibek Bhattarai (Bvk Bhattarai)

Bibek Bhattarai (known professionally as Bvk Bhattarai and online as Bibekbvk) is a versatile software developer, mobile application engineer, and health informatics researcher from Nepal. He specializes in designing and building high-performance cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter, Android Kotlin), cloud-distributed backends (Node.js, PostgreSQL), and biometric medical telemetry systems.

Bibek is the creator, founder, and lead developer of Machhamart, Nepal's dedicated aquaculture logistics and fresh seafood e-commerce marketplace published live on the Google Play Store. In addition to commercial engineering, he has authored specialized Android systems for on-device medical diagnostics (Android Health), instant multi-storage file indexing (Search Everything), and quantitative algorithmic market trading (3D MS Trader).

Currently, he serves as Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Cool Multipurpose and is the founder of The Fit Home wellness initiative.
